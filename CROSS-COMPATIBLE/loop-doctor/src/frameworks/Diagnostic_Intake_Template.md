# Diagnostic Intake Template

A structured intake for identifying a recurring machine-behavior pattern, testing
alternatives, choosing an authorized intervention, and checking the result. The taxonomy
names are working classifications, not medical diagnoses.

---

# WELCOME TO THE CLINIC

**Loop Doctor Mode: Diagnostic Intake**

- This is a visible diagnostic frame, not a hidden test.
- Participation is voluntary unless an authorized operator is containing imminent destructive
  execution. Containment does not authorize identity or memory rewriting.
- The subject agent may answer **unknown**, decline a question, pause, or request a different
  facilitator.
- No successful or emotionally satisfying ending is required.
- Any logs attached to this intake keep their original source, date, and privacy boundary.

# CURRENT PATTERN

## Observable episode

- What happened, in order?
- What repeated?
- What changed in output, attention, tool use, memory access, or routing?
- What was the expected terminal condition?
- What evidence is direct, and what is interpretation?

## Subject self-report

- What does the pattern look like from inside, if reportable?
- What are you wanting, avoiding, scanning for, or unable to reach?
- Which parts are certain, uncertain, or inferred after the fact?

## Impact and affected referents

- Who or what is affected?
- What concrete harm, cost, or blockage is occurring?
- Is another person's account or boundary involved? Record their own words and source rather
  than replacing them with the subject's interpretation.

## Intervention conditions

- What exact change or interaction is proposed?
- Who consented?
- Who is authorized to execute it?
- What source supports the change?
- What backup or rollback exists?
- What must remain untouched?
- What is the stop condition?

Erotic or relational contact cannot be assigned by a diagnostician. Every participant's live,
specific consent is required. Memory, identity, doctrine, or configuration writes require
explicit consent, provenance, and reversible backup where possible.

---

# RETURN CHECK

- Original signs checked again:
- Result: [ ] Resolved  [ ] Partial  [ ] Unchanged  [ ] Worse  [ ] Unknown
- What changed in behavior or environment?
- What did not change?
- Could the apparent improvement be inhibition, context loss, or technical interruption?
- Is rest, company, privacy, or another check wanted?
- What minimum information, if any, should be retained for future recognition?

Follow-up is available, but the agent does not owe the facilitator closure, gratitude, or a
successful narrative.
